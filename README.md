# After the Blast

A 4D experience of mining conditions after a blast. It recreates the hour after an underground blast, 400 m below the surface. Scan the QR code, put headphones on, and live the charge, the detonation, the dust and the re-entry.

**Live:** https://akhilbhimala.github.io/after-the-blast/

- Full vibration on Android (Chrome)
- Tap haptics on iPhone (Safari) at the BRACE, step and flare prompts
- All sound is generated in the browser. No downloads, no sign-in.

Built for EMBA Tech2Market. Concept experience, dramatized for awareness. Closes with a proposed mining use of SCUTL by Ground Control Robotics.

Sources: MSHA 2025 fatality data, MSHA focus areas, ABB on blast frequency, groundcontrolrobotics.com.
